Blerb Takes Flight for iPhone and iPad

How to play

  • Tap the title card to start.
  • Put a thumb down in the lower-left part of the screen and push it the way you want Blerb to go.
  • Tap Jump! to hop over the vacuum and reach FART! clouds.
  • Grab green FART! clouds to get your cape and fly. More clouds, faster flying.
  • Grab a golden bone to go into vacuum attack mode, then touch the vacuum to bite it.
  • When the FART! meter is full, press SUPER TOOT! to toot away everything nearby.
  • Beat each level's boss with three golden-bone bites.

Common questions

Which devices does it run on? iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later and an A12 chip or newer (iPhone XS / XR and later).

Does it need the internet? No. Blerb works completely offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No.

Where is my best score? On your device. Deleting the app resets it.
