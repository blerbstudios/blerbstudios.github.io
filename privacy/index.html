Privacy Policy

Blerb Takes Flight · Last updated November 3, 2026

Blerb Takes Flight is a game for kids, made by a kid and his dad. It does not collect any information about you.

  • Blerb does not ask for your name, email, age, or any other personal information.
  • Blerb does not use the internet. It has no ads, no accounts, no chat, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Blerb does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.
  • The only thing Blerb saves is your best score, and it stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Blerb is made for kids. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Sharing

Because Blerb collects nothing, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version of Blerb is released.
